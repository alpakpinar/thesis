\beginchapterabstract{Theoretical Background}

This chapter provides a concise overview of key topics in 
contemporary particle physics. It begins with a brief discussion
of the history of particle physics, tracing the development of key 
concepts and discoveries that have shaped the current understanding 
of the field. This is followed by an overivew
of the Standard Model of particle physics, outlining its fundamental 
particles and interactions. Following this, the chapter delves into the 
evidence for dark matter, discussing observational data and current experimental 
efforts aimed at dark matter detection. 
Finally, it offers a brief overview of Higgs portal models, which propose
interactions between the Standard Model Higgs boson and dark matter particles.
Through Higgs portal models, the chapter explains how such interactions can be
observed as invisible decays of the Higgs boson (commonly denoted as $\hinv$)
in particle detectors.

\finishchapterabstract
