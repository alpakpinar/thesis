\chapter{Results}
\label{chapter:results}

\begin{abstract}
    This chapter provides the results of the Vector Boson Fusion (VBF) $\hinv$ 
    analysis, for which the analysis strategy is presented in the previous chapter.
    No statistically significant excess in data compared to Standard Model (SM) background
    is observed, therefore the results are interpreted as an upper bound on the
    branching fraction of $\hinv$ decays.
    First part of this chapter focuses on the results obtained 
    from the 2017 and 2018 datasets. This is followed by a discussion of the results 
    obtained through combination with additional proton-proton collision data from 
    2016, and also with orthogonal event categories~\cite{VBFHinvAnalysisPaper}.
    With all available data combined, assuming Standard Model Higgs boson production
    cross section, an upper bound of $\brhinv < 0.18$ is obtained with $95\%$ CL.
    The chapter closes with a discussion of how this result can also be interpreted 
    as an upper bound in dark matter (DM) - nucleon scattering cross section, and how such
    interpretations complement the results from DM direct detection experiments.
\end{abstract}
